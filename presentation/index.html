  1. CEOhands out the work
  2. Intakereads the bill
  3. Collectorpulls bills and meter data
  4. Auditorchecks every line
  5. Filerdrafts the claims
  6. Approverasks the owner
  7. Negotiatorcalls or emails the provider
  8. Financerecords the money
  9. Monitorwatches the next bill
